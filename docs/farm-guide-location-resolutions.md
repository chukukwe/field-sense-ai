## Scope and limits

- Biriniwa is corrected to Birniwa using government spelling. Its numeric ID is unchanged and Biriniwa is retained as an alias, preserving saved selections.
- Kankari is interpreted as Kankara, supported by the 2022 study's explicit Northern Guinea sampling description. Kankia remains a separate LGA under the NAERLS Sudan default.
- Ife-Osun is expanded to the four Ife LGAs as a corroborated interpretation: the 2023 study selected all four within its rainforest sample. This does not establish a precise boundary for every farm.
- Ayamelum is an external inference. The 2024 study describes derived savanna with rainforest patches at Ifite-Ogwari, a site within Ayamelum. NAERLS itself gives no replacement zone. Local heterogeneity and the limits of extrapolation remain explicit in the UI.
- The Ebonyi study's Table 1a places sampled communities in Ishielu, Izzi and Ohaukwu under Derived Savanna. This corroborates the missing destination for those NAERLS exceptions. It is not a whole-LGA field survey, and broader differences between studies are not silently applied to other LGAs.
- Tsafe uses the official government administrative identity and the NAERLS exception context. The additional AJOL article supplied by the user returned HTTP 403 during inspection, so this change does not claim to have verified its contents. The Kaiama PDF was also unavailable; the government directory and NAERLS exception support the spelling reconciliation without attributing ecological findings to an unread PDF.

All 19 records can now receive the existing source-reviewed windows for their assigned broad zones, with conditions and provenance. This does not validate additional crops or restore any of the 277 uncited legacy calendar rows. The uncertainty gate remains implemented and tested for future unresolved records.

Current provenance totals: 755 direct source matches, 9 name normalizations, 1 source-default resolution, 8 corroborated interpretations, 1 external inference, 0 unresolved records. All 774 numeric location IDs are preserved. Independent local agronomist review and field validation are still outstanding.
